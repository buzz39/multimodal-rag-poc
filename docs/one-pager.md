# Multimodal Document Intelligence Platform

**AI-Powered Search Across Text, Images & Structured Data**

---

## The Problem

Enterprises accumulate massive volumes of operational documents — inspection reports, compliance records, field assessments — containing a mix of text, tables, images, and diagrams. Today, finding specific information requires manually sifting through hundreds of files. Image-based data (photos, schematics, visual evidence) is effectively unsearchable, creating blind spots in decision-making and compliance workflows.

## Target Industries

- **Telecommunications** — Tower inspections, site surveys, equipment audits
- **Utilities & Energy** — Infrastructure assessments, safety compliance, maintenance logs
- **Construction & Engineering** — Site reports, progress documentation, structural evaluations
- **Regulatory & Compliance** — Audit trails, environmental assessments, certification records

Any organization managing large repositories of documents with mixed text and visual content.

## Our Solution

An AI-powered document intelligence platform with a **hybrid-ready architecture** that ingests enterprise documents and enables **natural-language queries across both text and image content**. Users ask questions in plain English — through Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp, or a web interface — and receive precise, source-cited answers drawn from the full document corpus.

The platform starts with proven semantic search and scales to knowledge-graph-powered reasoning for complex cross-document queries — **from 5 documents to thousands without re-architecture**.

**Example queries:**
- *"Show all towers in Zone 3 below regulation height"*
- *"Find inspection reports with visible corrosion damage from the last 6 months"*
- *"Which sites are overdue for compliance review?"*
